name: Archive visibility policy
description: Why July statistics are member-visible while earlier protected months remain restricted
---

The July 2026 statistics archive is available to approved ordinary dashboard members, including the monthly summary and its related daily views and drill-downs. This is a targeted exception, not permission to open every previously protected month or bypass dashboard access controls.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for July statistics to be visible to ordinary users. Earlier restricted months were not included in that request.

**How to apply:** Keep month eligibility consistent across archive summaries, daily statistics, and signal-history drill-downs. Retain authentication and subscription checks.
