Stealth Breakout must freeze a valid local 4H quiet-zone resistance before a later closed 4H crossing. Same-cycle structure discovery and firing is invalid, a 1H-only cross is never sufficient, and an ARMED level must not move after creation. The level need not be the highest resistance; ABNeon is observational.

**Why:** The first full production-history replay retained 0 of 54 legacy Stealth Breakout signals, including all former profit and B.U. outcomes. This confirms the old cohort was generated by same-cycle/stateless discovery, but also means the strict ARMED fix has no historical evidence of useful signal retention.

**How to apply:** The original quiet-zone ARMED detector was later restored but is now retired for new trades in favor of 4H Range Breakout. Preserve frozen-level and historical audit evidence; do not require ABNeon readiness for Range arms. Evaluate detector versions and confirmation branches separately.

Legacy labels cannot be reused as the result of a later retest entry: every confirmation variant must own its actual entry time, fill price, and forward outcome window. If the reproducible closed cohort is too small, collect non-trading ARMED observations before choosing direct, retest, or hybrid confirmation.

A confirmed closed-4H cross remains a valid breakout fact for 24 hours across every frozen-level detection method. A closed-1H cross is not sufficient. Entry eligibility is evaluated from the latest closed 30m state: near-level may fire, extension waits for a retest, and a confirmed return below the level invalidates.

**Why:** A 10-minute decision grace was shorter than the scan cadence and rejected a fully valid RUNE breakout because evaluation arrived 86 seconds late. Blindly accepting old crosses remains unsafe when price has moved or rejected.

**How to apply:** Separate breakout validity from current entry quality. Breakout validity requires the exact prior/current closed 4H pair across the frozen level. Use the latest closed 30m candles only for entry quality; accept the exact 0…+2% band, wait for retest above +2%, and invalidate on a closed return below the level. Never qualify from 1H or 30m state.
